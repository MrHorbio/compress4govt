📝 SSC Signature — specific instructions

From the official SSC advisory:

  • Allowed image size: 10 KB to 20 KB in JPEG / JPG format.
  • Image dimensions: about 6.0 cm (width) × 2.0 cm (height) at a resolution of 300 DPI.
  • Application with blurred / miniature signature will be rejected.
  • Signature should be horizontally aligned.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How do I compress a photo for a government job application?

Upload your photo, set your desired DPI, pixel dimensions, or target file size, then click Compress. Download the result and upload it to the government job portal.

Is my photo safe? Do you store my files?

Yes, completely safe. All processing happens locally in your browser. Your photo is never uploaded to any server, never stored, and never shared.

What DPI should I use for government job photos?

Most Indian government job portals (SSC, UPSC, IBPS, RRB) require photos at 200 DPI. Some ask for 300 DPI. Always check your specific job notification.

How do I reduce my photo to 200 KB or 50 KB?

Select the 'Target size' mode, enter 200 (or 50) in the KB field, and click Compress. The tool automatically adjusts compression quality.

Can I use this tool without internet?

Yes! After your first visit, the page is cached by your browser. You can disconnect and continue compressing photos offline.

What is the photo size for SSC CGL 2025?

For SSC CGL 2025, the photo must be between 20 KB and 50 KB, in JPEG format, with dimensions of 100×120 pixels and a minimum resolution of 200 DPI. The signature must be 10 KB to 20 KB in JPEG format, about 6.0 cm × 2.0 cm at 300 DPI. Always verify with the official SSC notification.

What is the photo size for UPSC IAS application?

For UPSC IAS applications, the photo should be a recent passport-size photograph in JPEG format, between 20 KB and 300 KB, with a minimum of 350×350 pixels. The signature should be 20 KB to 300 KB in JPEG format.
