📋 How to compress a photo for government job forms

Follow these simple steps to resize your photo to meet SSC, UPSC, IBPS, RRB, or State PSC requirements:

  • Step 1: Upload your photo (JPEG, PNG, or WebP). Your file stays on your device.
  • Step 2: Set your requirements — DPI (usually 200 DPI), pixel dimensions (e.g., 800×600), or target file size (e.g., 200 KB or 50 KB).
  • Step 3: Click Compress & Resize and download your compressed photo.
  • Step 4: Upload the compressed photo to your government job portal.

This tool is perfect for passport size photos, signature resizing, and document scanning for online applications.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How do I compress a photo for a government job application?

Upload your photo, set your desired DPI (e.g., 200 DPI), pixel dimensions (e.g., 800×600), or target file size (e.g., 200 KB), then click Compress. The tool resizes your photo instantly in your browser. Download the result and upload it to the government job portal.

Is my photo safe? Do you store my files?

Yes, your photo is completely safe. Compress4Gov processes all files locally in your browser using JavaScript. Your photo is never uploaded to any server, never stored, and never shared. You can even disconnect from the internet after the page loads and the tool will still work.

What DPI should I use for government job photos?

Most Indian government job portals (SSC, UPSC, IBPS, RRB) require photos at 200 DPI. Some ask for 300 DPI. The standard pixel size is 800×600 pixels (or 4×3 cm). Always check your specific job notification for exact requirements.

How do I reduce my photo to 200 KB or 50 KB?

Select the 'Target size' mode, enter 200 (or 50) in the KB field, and click Compress. The tool automatically adjusts compression quality to meet your target size while keeping the best possible image quality.

Can I use this tool without internet?

Yes! After your first visit, the page is cached by your browser. You can disconnect from the internet, refresh the page, and continue compressing photos offline. No data is ever sent online.

Is Compress4Gov really free?

Yes, Compress4Gov is 100% free with no hidden charges, no sign-up, and no watermarks. We may show non-intrusive affiliate links to job preparation resources, but the core tool remains free forever.
